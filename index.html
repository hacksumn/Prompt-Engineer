    Evidence, without the theater
    This app builds prompts locally using structured templates. It does not call models, browse sources, or certify factual claims. Ask your target model to retrieve primary sources, test counterevidence, and disclose what it could not verify. For independent review, run the prompt in multiple models and compare their claims against original sources.
